    Phraseology¶

    Requesting and receiving taxi¶

    A taxi clearance will name the holding point for your departure runway and the route to get there. Always read back the runway and any hold short instruction.

    Phraseology

    CEB585: Ground, CEB585, request taxi runway 24.

    RPLL_GND: CEB585, taxi N, C, H1, hold short runway 24.

    CEB585: N, C, H1, hold short runway 24, CEB585.

    CEB585: Ground, CEB585, request taxi runway 24.

    RPLL_GND: CEB585, taxi N, C, hold short runway 24, follow company A320 ahead.

    CEB585: N, C, hold short runway 24, following company A320, CEB585.

    Holding Instructions (Mandatory Readback)¶

    Per ICAO Doc 4444, the words ROGER and WILCO are NOT sufficient when you are told to hold. You must read back with "HOLDING" or "HOLDING SHORT".

    Phraseology

    RPLL_GND: CEB585, hold short of runway 31.

    CEB585: Holding short runway 31, CEB585.

    RPLL_GND: CEB585, hold position.

    CEB585: Holding, CEB585.

    Crossing a runway¶

    Never enter or cross a runway until explicitly cleared. At RPLL, departures from 06/24 frequently cross runway 31.

    Phraseology

    RPLL_GND: CEB585, cross runway 31, report vacated.

    CEB585: Cross runway 31, wilco, CEB585.

    CEB585: CEB585, runway 31 vacated.

    RPLL_GND: CEB585, roger.

    Taking Off the Airport¶

    IFR aircraft departing runway 06 or 24 will be instructed to maintain runway heading and climb 7000’ initially after departure. While for aircraft departing runway 13 or 31 will be instructed to maintain runway heading and climb 3000’.

    If radar services are available, do not turn or climb further unless you are instructed to do so. If radar services are unavailable, you should only turn and climb further 3 minutes after you have departed.

    If you wish to take an intersection departure, let the controller know since controllers will normally give you a full length departure.

    Line up and wait¶

    Being cleared to line up and wait means enter the runway and hold in position. It is NOT a take-off clearance.

    Phraseology

    RPLL_TWR: CEB585, runway 24, line up and wait.

    CEB585: Runway 24, line up and wait, CEB585.

    RPLL_TWR: CEB585, behind landing A320 on short final, line up runway 24 behind.

    CEB585: Behind landing A320, line up runway 24 behind, CEB585.

    Take-off clearance¶

    Phraseology

    RPLL_TWR: CEB585, wind 240 degrees 8 knots, runway 24, cleared for take-off.

    CEB585: Runway 24, cleared for take-off, CEB585.

    RPLL_TWR: CEB585, runway 24, cleared for immediate take-off, traffic on final.

    CEB585: Runway 24, cleared for immediate take-off, CEB585.

    RPLL_TWR: CEB585, hold position, cancel take-off, I say again cancel take-off.

    CEB585: Holding, CEB585.

    RPLL_TWR: CEB585, stop immediately, CEB585 stop immediately.

    CEB585: Stopping, CEB585.

    After Departure (Climb and Turn Instructions)¶

    Once airborne under radar, follow the controller's heading and climb instructions.

    Phraseology

    RPLL_APP: CEB585, radar identified, turn right heading 1-2-0, climb 7000.

    CEB585: Right heading 1-2-0, climb 7000, CEB585.

    RPLL_APP: CEB585, climb via SID to FL130.

    CEB585: Climb via SID to FL130, CEB585.

    RPLL_APP: CEB585, resume own navigation, direct IBAGO, climb FL200.

    CEB585: Own navigation, direct IBAGO, climb FL200, CEB585.

    Climb, Cruise and Descent¶

    Levels are flown in accordance with the semi-circular rule noted at the top of this page. Always read back any level clearance in full. Level, heading and speed instructions are safety critical and require a readback.

    Phraseology

    MNL_CTR: PAL101, climb FL350.

    PAL101: Climb FL350, PAL101.

    MNL_CTR: PAL101, descend FL240, report passing FL290.

    PAL101: Descend FL240, wilco, PAL101.

    MNL_CTR: PAL101, when ready descend FL310.

    PAL101: When ready FL310, PAL101.

    MNL_CTR: PAL101, cross CAB at or above FL200.

    PAL101: Cross CAB at or above FL200, PAL101.

    Frequency changes / handoff¶

    Phraseology

    RPLL_APP: PAL101, contact Manila Control 125.5.

    PAL101: Manila Control 125.5, PAL101.

    MNL_CTR: PAL101, at NOMAN contact Hong Kong Radar 127.1.

    PAL101: At NOMAN contact Hong Kong Radar 127.1, PAL101.

    MNL_CTR: PAL101, monitor Manila Control 132.4.

    PAL101: Monitor 132.4, PAL101.

    Radar / Surveillance Services¶

    When a radar (ATS surveillance) service is available, expect identification, vectors and speed control.

    Identification and vectors¶

    Phraseology

    RPLL_APP: PAL101, radar contact, 15 miles north of Manila.

    PAL101: PAL101.

    RPLL_APP: PAL101, turn left heading 1-0-0, for spacing.

    PAL101: Left heading 1-0-0, PAL101.

    RPLL_APP: PAL101, resume own navigation, direct LAIYA.

    PAL101: Own navigation, direct LAIYA, PAL101.

    Speed control¶

    Phraseology

    RPLL_APP: PAL101, reduce speed 210 knots.

    PAL101: Speed 210 knots, PAL101.

    RPLL_APP: PAL101, maintain 250 knots or greater.

    PAL101: 250 knots or greater, PAL101.

    RPLL_APP: PAL101, no speed restrictions, resume normal speed.

    PAL101: Resume normal speed, PAL101.

    Traffic information¶

    Phraseology

    RPLL_APP: PAL101, traffic 2 o'clock, 10 miles, opposite direction, A320, FL370.

    PAL101: Looking out, PAL101.

    RPLL_APP: PAL101, traffic 11 o'clock, 8 miles, crossing left to right.

    PAL101: Traffic in sight, PAL101.

    Arrival and Approach¶

    Arrivals are typically cleared via a STAR and then for an approach.

    Phraseology

    RPLL_APP: PAL101, cleared Manila, LAIYA5R arrival, descend via STAR to FL110.

    PAL101: Cleared Manila, LAIYA5R arrival, descend via STAR to FL110, PAL101.

    RPLL_APP: PAL101, cleared ILS approach runway 24, report established.

    PAL101: Cleared ILS approach runway 24, wilco, PAL101.

    RPLL_APP: PAL101, report runway in sight.

    PAL101: Runway in sight, PAL101.

    RPLL_APP: PAL101, cleared visual approach runway 24.

    PAL101: Cleared visual approach runway 24, PAL101.

    Holding¶

    If you are held, expect a published or detailed holding clearance with an onward time.

    Phraseology

    RPLL_APP: PAL101, hold at CABANATUAN as published, expect approach clearance at time 45, maintain 6000ft.

    PAL101: Hold at CABANATUAN as published, maintain 6000ft, PAL101.

    RPLL_APP: PAL101, exit hold, cleared ILS approach runway 24.

    PAL101: Exit hold, cleared ILS approach runway 24, PAL101.

    Landing¶

    Phraseology

    RPLL_TWR: PAL101, wind 240 degrees 10 knots, runway 24, cleared to land.

    PAL101: Runway 24, cleared to land, PAL101.

    RPLL_TWR: PAL101, continue approach, prepare for possible go around.

    PAL101: Continue approach, PAL101.

    RPLL_TWR: PAL101, go around.

    PAL101: Going around, PAL101.

    After Landing¶

    Once clear of the runway, you will be told to contact Ground.

    Phraseology

    RPLL_TWR: PAL101, when vacated contact Ground 121.9.

    PAL101: When vacated, Ground 121.9, PAL101.

    RPLL_TWR: PAL101, expedite vacating, traffic 3 mile final.

    PAL101: Expediting, PAL101.
