# Phraseology Reference

---

## General

### Level Changes

!!! phraseology "Phraseology"

    === "Climb"

        **MNL_CTR**: PAL101, climb flight level 350.

        **PAL101**: Climb flight level 350, PAL101.

    === "Descend"

        **MNL_CTR**: PAL101, descend to flight level 240, report passing flight level 290.

        **PAL101**: Descend flight level 240, wilco, PAL101.

    === "Expedite"

        **MNL_CTR**: PAL101, expedite climb until passing flight level 130.

        **PAL101**: Expedite climb passing flight level 130, PAL101.

---

### Transfer of Control / Frequency Change

!!! phraseology "Phraseology"

    === "Standard"

        **RPLL_APP**: PAL101, contact Manila Control, 119.3
 
        **PAL101**: Manila Control 119.3, PAL101.

    === "At a Point"

        **MNL_CTR**: PAL101, at Noman contact Hong Kong Radar 127.1

        **PAL101**: At Noman contact Hong Kong Radar 127.1, PAL101.


---

### Traffic Information

!!! phraseology "Phraseology"

    === "Passing Traffic"

        **RPLL_APP**: RP-C603, traffic, southbound B737, flight level 110, estimated BATAY at 35.

        **RP-C603**: Looking out, RP-C603.

    === "No Traffic"

        **RPLL_APP**: RP-C603, no reported traffic.

        **RP-C603**: RP-C603.

---

## Clearance Delivery

### IFR Departure Clearance

!!! phraseology "Phraseology"

    === "Non-Radar"

        **CEB585**: Clearance Delivery, CEB585, Stand 120, A320 with information A, request clearance Mactan, runway 06.

        **RPLL_DEL**: CEB585, cleared Mactan W25 BATAY, runway 06, IPATA2R departure, climb 7000ft, squawk 4251.

        **CEB585**: Cleared Mactan W25 BATAY, runway 06, IPATA2R departure, climb 7000ft, squawk 4251, CEB585.

    === "Radar"

        **CEB585**: Clearance Delivery, CEB585, Stand 120, A320 with information A, request clearance Mactan, runway 06.

        **RPLL_DEL**: CEB585, cleared Mactan W25 BATAY, runway 06, fly runway heading, climb 7000ft, expect radar vectors IPATA, squawk 4251.

        **CEB585**: Cleared Mactan W25 BATAY, runway 06, fly runway heading, climb 7000ft, expect radar vectors IPATA, squawk 4251, CEB585.

---

### Startup Approval

!!! phraseology "Phraseology"

    === "Approved"

        **CEB585**: Clearance Delivery, CEB585, Stand 120, request start-up.

        **RPLL_DEL**: CEB585, start-up approved, QNH 1015.

        **CEB585**: Start-up approved, QNH 1015, CEB585.

    === "Expect Delay"

        **CEB585**: Clearance Delivery, CEB585, Stand 120, request start-up.

        **RPLL_DEL**: CEB585, expect start-up at time 45, traffic flow restriction, QNH 1015.

        **CEB585**: Wilco, QNH 1015, CEB585.

---

## Ground Control

### Pushback

!!! phraseology "Phraseology"

    === "Approved"

        **CEB585**: Ground, CEB585, Stand 120, request push and start.

        **RPLL_GND**: CEB585, push and start approved, starting point S18, facing east.

        **CEB585**: Push back approved, starting point S18, facing east, CEB585.

---

### Taxi

!!! phraseology "Phraseology"

    === "Standard Taxi"

        **CEB585**: Ground, CEB585, request taxi runway 24.

        **RPLL_GND**: CEB585, taxi N, C, H1, Hold short Runway 24

        **CEB585**: N, C, H1, Hold short Runway 24, CEB585.

    === "After Landing"

        **CEB105**: Ground, CEB105, vacated runway 24.

        **RPLL_GND**: CEB105, Taxi C, N, Stand 105.

        **CEB105**: C, N, Stand 105, CEB105.

---

## Tower (Aerodrome Control)

### Holding Instructions

!!! phraseology "Phraseology"

    === "Hold Short"

        **RPLL_TWR**: CEB585, hold short of runway 06, traffic on final.

        **CEB585**: Holding short runway 06, CEB585.

    === "Line Up"

        **RPLL_TWR**: CEB585, runway 06, line up and wait.

        **CEB585**: Runway 06, line up and wait, CEB585.

    === "Conditional Clearance"

        **RPLL_TWR**: CEB585, behind landing A-3-2-0 on short final, line up runway 06 behind.

        **CEB585**: Behind landing A-3-2-0, line up runway 06 behind, CEB585.

---

### Take-off Clearance

!!! phraseology "Phraseology"

    === "Immediate"

        **RPLL_TWR**: CEB585, wind 060 degrees, 8 knots runway 06, cleared for take-off.

        **CEB585**: Runway 06, cleared for take-off, CEB585.

    === "With Departure Instructions"

        **RPLL_TWR**: CEB585, fly runway heading climb 7000, runway 06, cleared for take-off.

        **CEB585**: fly runway heading climb 7000, runway 06, cleared for take-off, CEB585.

    === "Cancel Take-off"

        **RPLL_TWR**: CEB585, hold position, cancel take-off, I say again cancel take-off.

        **CEB585**: Holding, CEB585.

---

### Landing Clearance

!!! phraseology "Phraseology"

    === "Standard"

        **PAL101**: Tower, PAL101, ILS runway 24.

        **RPLL_TWR**: PAL101, wind 240 degrees, 10 knots, runway 24, cleared to land.

        **PAL101**: Runway 24, cleared to land, PAL101.

    === "With Caution"

        **RPLL_TWR**: PAL101, runway 24, cleared to land, wind 240 degrees, 10 knots, caution wake turbulence departing B777.

        **PAL101**: Runway 24, cleared to land, PAL101.

    === "Go Around"

        **RPLL_TWR**: PAL101, go around.

        **PAL101**: Going around, PAL101.

        **RPLL_TWR**: PAL101, fly runway heading, climb 7000

---

## Approach Control

### Departure Instructions (Radar)

!!! phraseology "Phraseology"

    === "Radar Vectors"

        **RPLL_APP**: CEB585, Radar identified, turn right heading 1-2-0, climb 7000.

        **CEB585**: Right heading 1-2-0, climb 7000, CEB585.

    === "Direct"

        **RPLL_APP**: CEB585, Direct IPATA, climb flight level 200.

        **CEB585**: Direct IPATA, climb flight level 200, CEB585.

---

### STAR Clearance / Approach Sequencing

!!! phraseology "Phraseology"

    === "STAR Clearance"

        **RPLL_APP**: PAL101, cleared Manila, LAIYA5R arrival, vectors ILS 06.

        **PAL101**: cleared Manila, LAIYA5R arrival, vectors ILS 06, PAL101.

    === "Speed Control"

        **RPLL_APP**: PAL101, reduce speed, 210 knots.

        **PAL101**: Speed 210 knots, PAL101.

    === "Heading"

        **RPLL_APP**: PAL101, Left heading 1-0-0

        **PAL101**: Left heading 1-0-0, PAL101

---

### Approach Clearance

!!! phraseology "Phraseology"

    === "ILS"

        **RPLL_APP**: PAL101, cleared ILS approach runway 06, report established.

        **PAL101**: Cleared ILS approach runway 06, wilco, PAL101.

    === "RNP"

        **RPLL_APP**: PAL101, at TRECE cleared RNP approach runway 06.

        **PAL101**: TRECE cleared RNP approach runway 06. PAL101.

    === "Visual"

        **RPLL_APP**: PAL101, report runway in sight.

        **PAL101**: Runway in sight, PAL101.

        **RPLL_APP**: PAL101, cleared visual approach runway 24.

        **PAL101**: Cleared visual approach runway 24, PAL101.

---

### Holding

!!! phraseology "Phraseology"

    === "Enter Hold"

        **RPLL_APP**: PAL101, hold WAYEM, expect further clearance at time 45, maintain 6000ft.

        **PAL101**: Hold WAYEM, maintain 6000ft, PAL101.

    === "Cleared for Approach from Hold"

        **RPLL_APP**: PAL101, exit hold, direct TRECE, cleared RNP approach runway 06.

        **PAL101**: Exit hold, direct TRECE, cleared RNP approach runway 06, PAL101.

---

## Area Control (En-Route)

### Issuance of Clearance (Departure from Non-Towered Aerodrome)

!!! phraseology "Phraseology"

    === "Clearance"

        **MNL_CTR**: PAL101, Cleared to join CAB, then as filed climb FL200, squawk 4010

        **PAL101**: Cleared to join CAB, then as filed, climb FL200, squawk 4010

---

### Separation Instructions

!!! phraseology "Phraseology"

    === "Cross at Time"

        **MNL_CTR**: PAL101, cross BATAY at time 32 or later.

        **PAL101**: Cross BATAY time 32 or later, PAL101.

    === "Maintain Mach"

        **MNL_CTR**: PAL101, maintain Mach 0.82 until NOMAN.

        **PAL101**: Maintain Mach 0.82 until NOMAN, PAL101.

---

### Emergency / Special Procedures

!!! phraseology "Phraseology"

    === "Minimum Fuel"

        **PAL101**: Manila Control, PAL101, minimum fuel.

        **MNL_CTR**: PAL101, roger, no delay expected, expect approach time 25.

        **PAL101**: PAL101.

    === "Mayday"

        **PAL101**: Mayday mayday mayday, Manila Control, PAL101, engine failure, maintaining flight level 350, request immediate descent and diversion to Manila.

        **MNL_CTR**: PAL101, roger mayday, descend immediately to flight level 150, direct BATAY, all traffic will be expedited for you, report souls on board and fuel remaining.

    === "TCAS RA"

        **PAL101**: TCAS RA, PAL101.

        **MNL_CTR**: PAL101, roger.

        *(After RA is completed)*

        **PAL101**: Clear of conflict, returning to flight level 330, PAL101.

        **MNL_CTR**: PAL101, roger.
