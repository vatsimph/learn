# Visual Flight Rules (VFR) Within Butuan ATZ

## VFR Area Chart

![VFR Area Chart RPME](../../../assets/img/RPME%20VFR/VFR%20Area%20Chart%20RPME.png)

## General Procedures for VFR Flights

The following air traffic procedures shall apply to all VFR flights when entering, leaving or operating within Butuan ATZ.

1. All arriving VFR flights shall contact Butuan Approach on 121.3 MHZ upon entering Butuan TMA.
2. All instructions and traffic information shall be acknowledged by pilots.
3. IFR flights shall have priority over VFR flights.
4. Any deviation from the procedures shall be advised without delay to Butuan ATC.

## VFR Departures

=== "RWY 12"

    **North Bound:** After airborne, turn left towards MAGALLANES shoreline. Report abeam CABADBARAN. Continue towards TUBAY SHORELINE. Report switching frequency or proceed as instructed by ATC.

    **South Bound:** After airborne, fly towards Agusan River. Turn right abeam MACAPAGAL BRIDGE. Keep to the West of Agusan River. Report over BIT-OS. Report over TUNGAO. Report switching frequency or proceed as instructed by ATC.

    **West Bound:** After airborne, make a left downwind departure towards BUENAVISTA shoreline. Request ATC clearance to cross final approach RWY 12. Continue westward and report abeam NASIPIT. Report abeam MAGSAYSAY. Report switching frequency or proceed as instructed by ATC.

    *If proceeding to Laguindingan Airport:* from MAGSAYSAY, continue climb VFR on a west-north-west direction towards Camiguin Island. Report switching frequency to Laguindingan Approach or proceed as instructed by ATC.

=== "RWY 30"

    **North Bound:** After airborne, turn right towards MAGALLANES shoreline. Continue to the North and report abeam CABADBARAN. Report TUBAY SHORELINE. Report switching frequency or proceed as instructed by ATC.

    **South Bound:** After airborne, make a right downwind departure towards Butuan CITY HALL. Request ATC clearance to cross final approach RWY 30 towards West of MACAPAGAL BRIDGE. Keep to the West of Agusan River and report over BIT-OS. Report over TUNGAO. Report switching frequency or proceed as instructed by ATC.

    **West Bound:** After airborne, turn left towards BUENAVISTA shoreline. Continue westward and report abeam NASIPIT. Report abeam MAGSAYSAY. Report switching frequency or proceed as instructed by ATC.

    *If proceeding to Laguindingan Airport:* from MAGSAYSAY, continue on a west-north-west direction towards South of Camiguin Island then turn left on a south-south-west direction towards Laguindingan Airport. Report switching to Laguindingan Approach or proceed as instructed by ATC.

## VFR Arrivals

=== "RWY 12"

    **From the North:** Report abeam TUBAY SHORELINE. Follow Butuan Bay coastline and report abeam CABADBARAN. Report MAGALLANES shoreline. Request ATC clearance to join left downwind RWY 12 or proceed as instructed by ATC.

    **From the South:** Report abeam BAYUGAN. Keep to the East side of Agusan River and report over MAGUINDA. Report abeam BIT-OS and request ATC clearance to cross final approach RWY 30 towards East of MACAPAGAL BRIDGE and to join left downwind RWY 12 or proceed as instructed by ATC.

    **From the West:** Report abeam ODIONGAN. Follow Gingoog coastline and report abeam MAGSAYSAY. Turn right towards Butuan Airport and report over NASIPIT. Report approaching BUENAVISTA and request ATC clearance for a straight-in approach RWY 12 or proceed as instructed by ATC.

=== "RWY 30"

    **From the North:** Report abeam TUBAY SHORELINE. Follow Butuan Bay coastline and report abeam CABADBARAN. Report MAGALLANES shoreline. Request ATC clearance to join right base RWY 30 or proceed as instructed by ATC.

    **From the South:** Report abeam BAYUGAN. Keep to the East side of Agusan River and report over MAGUINDA. Report approaching MACAPAGAL BRIDGE and request ATC clearance for a straight-in approach RWY 30 or proceed as instructed by ATC.

    **From the West:** Report abeam ODIONGAN. Follow Gingoog coastline and report abeam MAGSAYSAY. Turn right towards Butuan Airport and report over NASIPIT. Continue towards BUENAVISTA. Request ATC clearance to cross final approach RWY 30 and join right downwind RWY 30 or proceed as instructed by ATC.

## Traffic Circuit

=== "RWY 12"

    ![RPME Traffic Circuit RWY 12](../../../assets/img/RPME%20VFR/Traffic%20Circuit%20RWY%2012.png)

    **Left-hand circuit.** Aircraft at **105 KT or less** fly the inner circuit (1.0 NM from the runway); aircraft at **106 KT or more** fly the outer circuit (2.0 NM).

=== "RWY 30"

    ![RPME Traffic Circuit RWY 30](../../../assets/img/RPME%20VFR/Traffic%20Circuit%20RWY%2030.png)

    **Right-hand circuit.** Aircraft at **105 KT or less** fly the inner circuit (1.0 NM from the runway); aircraft at **106 KT or more** fly the outer circuit (2.0 NM).

!!! info "Circuit entry and exit"

    - **Arriving aircraft** shall enter the traffic circuit on the **downwind leg at an angle of 45 degrees**.
    - **Departing aircraft** shall follow the traffic circuit and **leave the circuit at an angle of 45 degrees**.
