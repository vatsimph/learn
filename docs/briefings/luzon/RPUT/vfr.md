# Visual Flight Rules (VFR) at Tuguegarao

The traffic circuit charts for Tuguegarao Airport are available in the **Charts** section of the [aerodrome page](index.md). This page summarises the published traffic circuit.

## General Procedures for VFR Flights

1. Maintain a listening watch on **Tuguegarao Radio (122.8 MHZ)**, or on **UNICOM (122.8 MHZ)** when the FSS is not online, and self-announce your position and intentions.
2. A maximum of **two (2) aircraft** are permitted in the traffic pattern at any given time.
3. Maintain a good lookout at all times; there is no radar service.

## Traffic Circuit

=== "RWY 17"

    ![RPUT Traffic Circuit RWY 17](../../../assets/img/RPUT%20VFR/Traffic%20Circuit%20RWY%2017.png)

    **Right-hand circuit.**

    - Aircraft with a pattern speed of **105 KT or less** fly the inner circuit (1.0 NM from the runway).
    - Aircraft at **106 KT or more** fly the outer circuit (2.0 NM from the runway).

=== "RWY 35"

    ![RPUT Traffic Circuit RWY 35](../../../assets/img/RPUT%20VFR/Traffic%20Circuit%20RWY%2035.png)

    **Left-hand circuit.**

    - Aircraft with a pattern speed of **105 KT or less** fly the inner circuit (1.0 NM from the runway).
    - Aircraft at **106 KT or more** fly the outer circuit (2.0 NM from the runway).

!!! info "Circuit entry and exit"

    - **Arriving aircraft** shall enter the traffic circuit on the **downwind leg at an angle of 45 degrees**.
    - **Departing aircraft** shall follow the traffic circuit and **leave the circuit at an angle of 45 degrees**.
